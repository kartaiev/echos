# Mode Variant data follows Melling, mapped onto Heirmologic and Sticheraric

The first source we found that gives Genus, Base and Dominant Degrees for all eight Modes in one place is David J. Melling, "Reading Psalmodia" (2000), §9. Melling does not always split melodies by Melos: for several Modes he separates "Irmic and short Sticheric" melodies from "slow Sticheric" ones instead. We map the first group to Heirmologic and the second to Sticheraric. This gives Plagal 2nd Heirmologic in Soft Chromatic and Plagal 2nd Sticheraric in Hard Chromatic, which agrees with ADR-0002.

Where Melling offers alternatives, we take the first one he lists:
- **Bases.** "Vu or Di", "Pa or Ke" and "Zo or Zo'" each give the first.
- **Plagal 4th.** He gives an Octave-System mode on Ni and a Tetrachordal one on Ga, and says both serve all styles, so both Melos get Ni. Some teachers put Heirmologic on Ga.
- **Grave.** He gives the Enharmonic on Ga for the short styles, and Diatonic modes on the low Zo for short and slow styles. Heirmologic takes the Enharmonic on Ga and Sticheraric the Diatonic on low Zo. The Octave-System Enharmonic on a lowered Zo is left out.
- **3rd.** He gives the Enharmonic on Ga for both kinds of melody, so the Diatonic 3rd mentioned in ADR-0002 is not in release 1.

The 4th Mode's common Soft Chromatic short style is also left out, since his table lists Legetos first.

Teachers disagree on several of these choices, most of all on the 2nd, Plagal 2nd and Plagal 4th. Every Mode Variant therefore stays `unreviewed` (ADR-0001) until a reviewer signs it off, and changing any of these choices is a data change only. Cadences and the Intonation come with the tickets that need them.
