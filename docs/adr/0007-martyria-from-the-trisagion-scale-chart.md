# Martyria follow the Trisagion scale chart, drawn with the Neanes font

Each Degree on the ladder shows its Martyria: the Degree's letter, the root sign written with it, and in the Enharmonic a Fthora. The values come from the Trisagion School "The Scales" chart, the same chart the interval sizes come from (ADR-0001). The chart prints a Martyria next to every Degree of the four Genera.

The chart covers low Zo to Ni' in the Diatonic, low Ke to Vou' in the Hard Chromatic, Ni to Pa' in the Soft Chromatic and Ni to Vou' in the Enharmonic. The ladder runs from low Ke to high Ga', so the Degrees beyond the chart follow its own pattern:
- the Diatonic repeats the octave's sign;
- the two Chromatic Genera keep alternating their two signs;
- the Enharmonic keeps its three-Degree cycle above Ga and takes the Diatonic signs below Ni, as the chart does for Ni, Pa and Vou.

The scorewriter Neanes gives the same signs for the Diatonic and both Chromatic Genera. Its Enharmonic on Ga differs from the chart for Ni, Pa and Vou; we follow the chart, since it is what the reviewer's school teaches. Neanes code is GPL-3.0, so none of it is used; only its font is.

The signs are drawn with the Neanes font in the SBMuFL layout (SIL OFL 1.1). Root signs are zero-width marks that the font places under the letter before them, or over it for low Ke and below.

Every table stays `unreviewed` until a reviewer signs it off, and changing a sign is a data change only.
