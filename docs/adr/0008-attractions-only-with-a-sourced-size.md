# Attractions only where a source gives their size

An Attraction is stored with the Tuning System only when a source gives its size in moria. For now that is one: in the Diatonic, Zo' (the Zo above Ke) is lowered by 4 moria. The Trisagion School course Octoëchos 201 gives it as Ke to Zo' 10 moria natural and 6 moria flat, for every Diatonic Mode.

The same course describes other Attractions, such as Vou pulled towards Pa or Ga in the 1st Mode and Pa raised towards Vou in the Plagal 4th. It calls them slight and, on purpose, gives no size: it says their degree is oral tradition and differs between schools. We do not invent sizes for them; each one can be added as data once a source gives a number.

Attractions are manual toggles (release 1, #1), offered only in the Genus they belong to. They switch off when the Genus or the Mode Variant changes, and a sounding Ison on the moved Degree moves with it. The value stays `unreviewed` (ADR-0001) until a reviewer signs it off.
