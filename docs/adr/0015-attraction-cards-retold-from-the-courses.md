# Attraction Cards retold from the courses

The Attractions Deck asks how a Degree is sung in a situation of the melody: "1st Mode: the melody comes down through Vou to Pa. How is Vou sung here?" Answer: slightly lowered. The rules come from the Trisagion School courses (Octoëchos 201 and 203, ByzB 103) and are kept as sourced data in `src/core/theory/attractionRules.ts`, each rule with the lesson it comes from (ADR-0010). The situations are retold in our own words; no course text is copied, since the courses are paid material.

An answer says only which way the Degree goes (natural, lowered, raised) and whether the course calls the move slight. Only Zo has a size, the one a source gives (ADR-0008): 10 moria above Ke natural, 6 lowered, read off the Tuning System. The others stay without a number, as the courses leave them to oral tradition. Where a course says some chanters make the move and others do not, the answer says so.

The Vou of the Grave Mode, which the courses put a half step under Ga, has no Card: the Grave Mode here is Enharmonic on Ga, whose Scale already sets Vou there, so it is not an Attraction in this app.
