# Cadences follow from the Base and the Dominant Degrees

ADR-0002 gave every Mode Variant Cadences, classed as complete, incomplete or final, but neither Melling nor the ByzB-101 chart lists them. The Trisagion School course Octoëchos 201 ("Melodic Formulas/Cadences") defines the classes instead of listing them: a complete Cadence ends on the Base, an incomplete one on another structural note, and a final one ends a piece.

So the Cadences are not stored: they are derived from each Mode Variant's Base and Dominant Degrees, and cite the course. Final Cadences are left out, since the course names no Degree for them. Some Mode Variants have rarer structural notes the course mentions (Ga and Ke in 1st Mode Heirmologic, Di in 1st Mode Sticheraric); they are not Dominant Degrees in our data, so they are not shown as Cadences either.

Like the data they come from, the Cadences stay `unreviewed` (ADR-0001).
