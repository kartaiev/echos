# Tempo Presets follow Melling's table of tempo signs

The Tempo Presets come from David J. Melling, "Reading Psalmodia" (2000), §11 Rhythm & Tempo, the only source we found that gives each tempo sign a range in beats per minute. Melling himself calls the table "very approximate guidelines". The Trisagion materials we use for the Heirmologic Mode Variants (ADR-0005) give no tempo table.

We take his names and ranges, with two readings:
- **Mesi** is printed as "100 - 100". We read it as 100 to 120, up to where Metria begins, since every other range meets the next one.
- **Chyma**, the speech tempo, is chanted near a monotone without a beat, so it is not a Tempo Preset.

Choosing a preset sets the slowest tempo of its range. Melling notes that Mesi and Metria are sometimes swapped in use, and the 1881 Patriarchal Committee is said to have fixed its own ranges for these signs, which we have not seen. Every preset therefore stays `unreviewed` (ADR-0001) until a reviewer signs it off, and changing a name or range is a data change only.
