# Neume names and intervals in two Decks

ADR-0014 put the names of the Neumes and the intervals they write in one Deck, names first. A Review gives ten new Cards, so a student's first Review was all names. The intervals came only after that. One Deck also asked two different questions, and a student could not tell which kind of Card would come next.

So they are now two Decks, each asking one thing:

- **Neumes**: the ten names ("Which Neume is this?").
- **Intervals**: the 39 written intervals ("Where does it take the melody?"), the single signs among them.

The Card ids stay as they were (`neume:…`, `interval:…`), so a student keeps what they have learned.

New interval Cards come simplest first, not in the order of the handout (up seven down to down seven):

1. One note written with one sign: Ison, Oligon, Kentimata, Apostrofos, Elafron, Chamili.
2. One note written with several signs: the Ison or Apostrofos with the Oligon, the Kentima and Ypsili over it, the Chamili with the Elafron and Apostrofos.
3. Two notes: Yporroi, Continuous Elafron, and the Kentimata combinations.
4. The Petasti forms, an ornament.

Within each, a sign with a name of its own comes first, in the order of the names. After that, nearer intervals come before further ones, and up before down.
