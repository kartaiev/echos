# Echos

A free practice companion for students of Byzantine chant: scales, modes, ison and tempo, built on the Chrysanthine (New Method) theory. Interface languages are English and Greek.

## Language

### Pitch and tuning

**Moria**:
The unit of interval size in Byzantine theory (plural and singular are the same). In the default Tuning System an octave is 72 moria.
_Avoid_: cents, microtones, steps
_Greek_: μόρια

**Tuning System**:
A named, sourced table of interval sizes in moria for every Genus (e.g. Patriarchal Music Committee 1881, 72 moria; Chrysanthos, 68 moria).
_Avoid_: temperament, tuning
_Greek_: σύστημα (e.g. Πατριαρχική Μουσική Επιτροπή 1881)

**Degree**:
One of the seven named positions of the scale: Ni, Pa, Vou, Ga, Di, Ke, Zo, repeated in lower and upper octaves.
_Avoid_: note, step, pitch, Bou, Gha, Dhi
_Greek_: φθόγγος; Νη, Πα, Βου, Γα, Δι, Κε, Ζω

**Reference Pitch**:
The pitch the user assigns to Ni, as a Western note plus a fine adjustment in hertz; with the Register it fixes the frequency of Ni, and every other sounding pitch is derived from that.
_Avoid_: key, tonic, concert pitch
_Greek_: τονικό ύψος του Νη

**Register**:
Male or female voice; the female Register sounds one octave above the male one.
_Avoid_: voice type, octave setting
_Greek_: φωνή: ανδρική, γυναικεία

**Genus**:
A family of interval patterns: Diatonic, Soft Chromatic, Hard Chromatic or Enharmonic.
_Avoid_: scale type, mode, gamut
_Greek_: γένος: Διατονικό, Μαλακό χρωματικό, Σκληρό χρωματικό, Εναρμόνιο

**Scale**:
A concrete ladder of Degrees with interval sizes, produced by applying a Genus within a Tuning System.
_Avoid_: mode, gamut
_Greek_: κλίμακα

**Attraction**:
A temporary raising or lowering of a Degree by a neighbouring Degree (e.g. Zo lowered when descending in the 1st Mode).
_Avoid_: accidental, sharp, flat, elxis
_Greek_: έλξη (lowered: ύφεση, raised: δίεση)

**Fthora**:
A sign that switches the Scale to another Genus, or to another Degree's place, at a Degree (plural: Fthores). The Degree it is placed on, the pivot, keeps its pitch and becomes the Degree the Fthora names, in the Fthora's Genus; the whole Scale follows and is sung under the new names (ADR-0011, ADR-0012). Fthores chain: each acts on the Scale the ones before it made.
_Avoid_: phthora, modulation sign
_Greek_: φθορά, φθορές

**Pivot**:
The Degree a Fthora is placed on; it keeps its pitch while the Scale around it changes.
_Avoid_: anchor, root
_Greek_: φθόγγος της φθοράς

**Neume**:
Any symbol of Byzantine notation: intervallic, qualitative or rhythmic.
_Avoid_: note, sign, glyph
_Greek_: σημάδι, χαρακτήρας

**Martyria**:
A sign that names the Degree and Genus at a point in the score (plural: Martyries).
_Avoid_: key signature, clef
_Greek_: μαρτυρία, μαρτυρίες

### Modes

**Mode**:
One of the eight: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, Plagal 1st, Plagal 2nd, Grave, Plagal 4th.
_Avoid_: Echos, Tone, Glas, Plagal of the First, Varys
_Greek_: ήχος: Ήχος Α΄, Β΄, Γ΄, Δ΄, Πλάγιος του Α΄, Πλάγιος του Β΄, Βαρύς, Πλάγιος του Δ΄

**Melos**:
The style of chanting a text: Heirmologic, Sticheraric or Papadic.
_Avoid_: genre, chant style
_Greek_: μέλος: Ειρμολογικό, Στιχηραρικό, Παπαδικό

**Mode Variant**:
A Mode as sung in one Melos, with its own Base, Genus, Dominant Degrees and Cadences (e.g. Plagal 2nd Heirmologic is Soft Chromatic, Plagal 2nd Sticheraric is Hard Chromatic).
_Avoid_: sub-mode, mode version
_Greek_: ήχος ανά μέλος (e.g. Πλάγιος του Β΄ Ειρμολογικός)

**Base**:
The home Degree of a Mode Variant.
_Avoid_: tonic, root, finalis
_Greek_: βάση

**Dominant Degree**:
A Degree of structural weight in a Mode Variant, where melodies tend to rest or turn.
_Avoid_: reciting tone
_Greek_: δεσπόζων φθόγγος

**Cadence**:
A resting point of a Mode Variant on a specific Degree, classed as incomplete, complete or final.
_Avoid_: ending
_Greek_: κατάληξη: ατελής, εντελής, τελική

**Intonation**:
The short sung formula that announces a Mode Variant before a hymn.
_Avoid_: apichima, apechema, echema
_Greek_: απήχημα

### Practice

**Ison**:
A sustained tone on a Degree held under the melody.
_Avoid_: drone, pedal
_Greek_: ίσον

**Ison Voice**:
The vowel the Ison is sung on (a, e, o or ou) and its volume, chosen by the student so it blends with their own voice.
_Avoid_: drone sound, timbre
_Greek_: φωνή του ίσου

**Parallage**:
Singing a melody or scale on Degree names instead of the text.
_Avoid_: solfège, solfa
_Greek_: παραλλαγή

**Chronos**:
The basic unit of time against which note durations are counted (plural: chronoi).
_Avoid_: beat, pulse
_Greek_: χρόνος, χρόνοι

**Tempo Preset**:
A named tempo range of the tradition (e.g. Bradeia, Mesi, Metria) expressed in chronoi per minute.
_Avoid_: speed
_Greek_: αγωγή: Βραδεία, Μέση, Μετρία, Ταχεία, Ταχυτάτη

### Study

**Card**:
A question on one sign of the notation (a Neume, a Fthora, a Martyria) with its answer. The student answers in mind, turns the Card, and says whether they remembered; a Card remembered comes back later and later (ADR-0013).
_Avoid_: flashcard, quiz, question
_Greek_: κάρτα

**Deck**:
The Cards on one kind of sign: Neumes (their names and the intervals they write), Fthores or Martyries.
_Avoid_: set, collection, topic
_Greek_: σειρά καρτών (named by its sign: Χαρακτήρες, Φθορές, Μαρτυρίες)

**Review**:
One pass through a Deck: the Cards due today, then a few new ones.
_Avoid_: session, lesson, practice (Practice is the singing view)
_Greek_: επανάληψη
